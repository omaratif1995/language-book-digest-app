# Scope, competence and exam claims

Keep three claims separate:

| Claim | Needed evidence |
|---|---|
| source inspected and learning value compressed | complete internal source map, selection review, accurate three-section sheet |
| learner can use the language | successful understanding, recall and original spoken/written use with feedback |
| ready for a named exam | current official tasks/criteria, authentic listening/speaking and assessed performance |

A compact sheet can replace rereading the lesson’s teaching content; it cannot by itself prove competence or readiness for every B1 exam.

For a whole book, internally reconcile all lesson, module, partner and reference pages before claiming the PDF is processed. Do not display that map in learner HTML.

Missing audio/video/transcripts remain internal limitations and are disclosed in the handoff. Do not invent listening content or place warning panels in the sheet. A print-based sheet may still be reviewed for the supplied print scope.

Exam-specific supplements, practice tasks and performance feedback belong in a separate artifact when requested. They do not alter the three-section lesson-sheet format.
